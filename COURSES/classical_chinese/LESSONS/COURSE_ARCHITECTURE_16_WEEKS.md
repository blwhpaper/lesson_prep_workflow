## Architecture Role

This artifact sits between the material matrix and the later package-standard layer.

Its function is to:

1. convert 16 material sessions into a coherent course progression
2. group the 16 weeks into teachable units
3. define competency domains and boundary conditions
4. create an acceptance frame for later prompt, package, assessment, and AI-method tasks
5. surface unresolved evidence and build dependencies without hiding them
