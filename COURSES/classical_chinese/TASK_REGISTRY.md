## Registry Rules

1. `TASK-CLCH-GOV-*` controls boundary, routing, and anti-drift governance only.
2. `TASK-CLCH-MAT-*` may structure source-derived course material below lesson level, but may not generate formal lesson artifacts.
3. `TASK-CLCH-DES-*` may shape teaching architecture only after the `16-session core material matrix` exists.
4. `TASK-CLCH-AI-*` belongs to pedagogy and workflow method, never to disciplinary content substitution.
5. `TASK-CLCH-ASSESS-*` begins only after content and design boundaries are stable enough for assignment suitability review.
