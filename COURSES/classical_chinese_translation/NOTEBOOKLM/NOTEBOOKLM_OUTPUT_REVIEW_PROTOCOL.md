# NotebookLM Output Review Protocol

For each output, verify prompt/task identity, source coverage, quotation traceability, page or chapter locators, unsupported claims, copyright exposure, and hallucination risk. Then assess reliability, course adaptation, closed-book suitability, AI-task suitability, and required human review.

Approved extracts may move only to L1 candidate records with links back to the raw output and audit evidence. Output cannot move directly into lessons, pathways, student tasks, question banks, or examinations.
