# Copyright And Source Boundary

1. Do not publish full textbooks, full CNKI papers, or full copyrighted translations.
2. Do not publish NotebookLM output containing substantial copyrighted text.
3. A public repository may contain original structures, prompts, short quotations, source indexes, teaching-task templates, and audit conclusions.
4. Copyright-sensitive material belongs in local or private paths protected by `.gitignore`.
5. `SOURCES/raw/`, `SOURCES/licensed/`, and `NOTEBOOKLM/OUTPUTS/` are sensitive by default.
6. A public GitHub repository must not contain substantial copyrighted text.

Unclear quotation limits, licenses, or redistribution rights require human review before publication.
