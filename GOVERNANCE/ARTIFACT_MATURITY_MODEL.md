# Artifact Maturity Model

| level | name | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| L0 | Raw Source | unprocessed source or tool output |
| L1 | Candidate Extraction | extracted candidate awaiting full audit |
| L2 | Source-Audited Material | traceable and reliability-reviewed |
| L3 | Course-Core Material | adapted and accepted for a named course |
| L4 | Lesson-Ready Material | lesson context, use, and scaffolding approved |
| L5 | Assessment-Ready Material | assessment validity and source suitability approved |
| L6 | Reusable Shared Pattern | generalized, decontextualized, and approved across courses |

- L0 and L1 must not enter lesson plans.
- L0, L1, and L2 must not enter closed-book examinations.
- Only L5 may enter an examination.
- Only L6 may enter cross-course shared templates or patterns.
- NotebookLM output starts only as L0 or L1.
- Prompt templates are workflow-control artifacts. Their `maturity_label` governs review strictness and publication boundary, but it does not convert them into lesson or assessment material.
- Promotion may not skip any level.

Promotion is explicit, documented, and reversible when new evidence appears.
