# Source Authority Protocol

Record source type, creator, title, edition/version, publisher or host, date, locator, access conditions, and audit status. Prefer primary or authoritative disciplinary sources; label secondary summaries and generated outputs accordingly.

No excerpt is traceable until a reviewer can locate it. Conflicting sources remain unresolved until adjudicated. Fabricated or unverifiable citations are prohibited. Reliability grades must include reasons and must not be inferred from writing quality.
