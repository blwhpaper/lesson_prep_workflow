# NotebookLM Output Audit

## Skill purpose

Determine whether raw NotebookLM output stays within source and copyright boundaries and may enter candidate extraction.

## When to use

After an output is saved in a course's `NOTEBOOKLM/OUTPUTS/`, before any material-database entry.

## Required inputs

Authorized task card, course SOT, prompt version, raw output, source list, and output-review protocol.

## Required outputs

A completed review record, cited defects, copyright flags, proposed maturity no higher than L1, and human-review items.

## Forbidden actions

Do not repair citations by guessing, promote directly to L2+, write lessons, create questions, or publish sensitive output.

## Fail-closed conditions

Missing prompt identity, source coverage, locator, rights boundary, task authorization, or material ownership.

## Acceptance checks

Every claim sample is traceable; unsupported claims and long quotations are flagged; review route and promotion decision are explicit.
