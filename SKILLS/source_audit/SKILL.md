# Source Audit

## Skill purpose

Establish whether a candidate material item has an authentic, locatable, authoritative, and legally usable source.

## When to use

Before promoting L1 candidate extraction to L2 source-audited material.

## Required inputs

Candidate record, source artifact or authoritative catalog record, bibliographic metadata, locator, and course source policy.

## Required outputs

Audit record, corrected citation if evidenced, reliability rationale, rights boundary, discrepancies, and pass/block decision.

## Forbidden actions

Do not invent metadata, infer locators, conceal conflicts, reproduce prohibited full text, or decide course adaptation.

## Fail-closed conditions

Unavailable source, unverifiable edition, ambiguous quotation boundary, unresolved conflict, or unknown redistribution rights.

## Acceptance checks

Another reviewer can locate the passage; metadata and locator agree; conflicts and copyright status are recorded; promotion decision is justified.
