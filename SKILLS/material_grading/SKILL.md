# Material Grading

## Skill purpose

Grade audited material for reliability, difficulty, course fit, teaching use, assessment use, AI review, and datafication.

## When to use

After source audit and before promotion to course-core, lesson-ready, or assessment-ready status.

## Required inputs

L2 record, source audit, course SOT, learner profile, maturity rules, and intended use.

## Required outputs

Field-complete material record, grades with reasons, approved uses, prohibited uses, maturity recommendation, and human-review flags.

## Forbidden actions

Do not override source defects, inflate maturity, force quotas, or treat novelty or fluent prose as reliability.

## Fail-closed conditions

Missing audit, unclear learner fit, undefined grading scale, contradictory intended uses, or unresolved disciplinary dispute.

## Acceptance checks

All schema fields are addressed; grades cite evidence; course and assessment decisions are separate; maturity follows governance rules.
